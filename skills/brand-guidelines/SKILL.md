ARC BRAND BOOK
WEB DESIGN SUMMARY

Brand direction
Arc should feel bold, sleek, restrained, clean, minimal, and dynamic. Combine understated luxury with advanced technology and a strong connection to life on the water.

Voice and copy
Use confident, clear, and approachable language. Keep sentences short and specific. Explain technology through its benefit to people and their experience. Avoid hype, jargon, vague claims, and pretentious language.

Use sentence case for headlines, subheadlines, buttons, navigation, and labels. Headlines do not need periods. Use "Arc" rather than "ARC" or "Arc Boats." Refer to products as "the Arc One" and "the Arc Sport." Use "100% electric" or "all-electric," and use "boat" in public-facing copy.

Color
Keep the interface clean and restrained with black, white, and cool grey. Use sand for backgrounds, Wake Blue for depth or supporting accents, and Signal Orange sparingly for key accents or calls to action.

Primary colors
White #FFFFFF
Black #1D1D1E
Cool Grey #646B73 for text only
Sand #EDEDE8
Wake Blue #123B4A
Signal Orange #FF6124

Use color with purpose, not decoration. Maintain WCAG AA contrast, with at least 4.5 to 1 for normal text and 3 to 1 for large text and interface elements. Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.

Logo
Use the brand mark in compact spaces and the wordmark when there is enough horizontal room or when the full name needs emphasis. Keep logos black or white, preserve clear space, maintain a minimum digital height of 16 pixels, and ensure strong contrast over images. Never stretch logos, add drop shadows, crowd them, or stack logo elements.

Typography
Use Söhne across the site. Use Söhne Kräftig for large headlines and Söhne Buch for body copy. Use Inter Semibold and Inter Regular when Söhne is unavailable. Keep text primarily left aligned and limit each composition to one or two weights.

Build hierarchy through contrast and restraint. Use bold, large headlines with concise body copy. Start digital type scales around a 16 pixel base and use a consistent scale rather than arbitrary sizes.

Imagery
Use large, high-quality photography that communicates performance, innovation, freedom, and purpose. Keep the boat as the focus and separate it from busy backgrounds. Use wide environmental shots to establish place and close shots to show craftsmanship. Prefer natural light, dawn or dusk glow, and candid documentary-style people photography over posed scenes.

Layout
Start with a modular 12 by 12 grid. Use generous negative space, strong alignment, large photography, and bold typography. Keep layouts uncluttered and responsive across screen sizes. Use the grid to create clear, dynamic sections without sacrificing readability.

Web design checklist
Use clear benefit-led copy.
Use the restrained Arc palette with orange accents only where needed.
Use accessible contrast and meaningful labels.
Use approved logos, fonts, imagery, and a modular grid.
Keep the overall page polished, spacious, and easy to scan.
